The Method

Nano Banana Pro — one designer, a full production line.

Most brand teams bottleneck between design, storefront build, and paid media — three different people, three handoffs, three delays. I built a pipeline that connects Claude to Figma, Shopify, and Meta Ads directly, so a single creative brief moves from concept to a live, ad-ready asset without leaving the system.

It's not "AI replacing a team." It's a brand designer who no longer waits on one. Every output is still art-directed, still on-brand, still mine — just shipped at a speed a five-person agency would struggle to match.

  • Input Creative brief / campaign ask
  • Orchestration Claude + MCP routing
  • Surfaces Figma → Shopify → Meta Ads → Email
  • Output On-brand, ad-ready, live in hours
